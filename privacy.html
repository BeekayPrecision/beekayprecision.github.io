Information we receive

When you contact Beekay Precision, you may provide your name, company, email address, telephone number, product requirements, drawings or other technical and commercial information.

How we use it

We use the information to assess and respond to your enquiry, prepare technical or commercial proposals, communicate about the requested work and maintain relevant business records.

Sharing and retention

Information is not intended to be sold. It may be shared with service providers or professional advisers where reasonably necessary to operate the business or respond to the enquiry. Records are retained only as long as needed for legitimate business, contractual or legal purposes.

Confidential technical information

Please do not send highly confidential drawings or intellectual property through ordinary email before suitable confidentiality arrangements are agreed. Contact us first if an NDA is required.

Your choices
