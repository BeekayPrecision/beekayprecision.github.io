Our company

From tooling knowledge to complete product thinking

Beekay Precision has developed expertise in precision press tools and plastic moulds over the years. That manufacturing foundation, combined with an understanding of electrical and electronics technology, led us toward the development of electrical products.

We aim to work as an extension of the customer’s design team. Whether the requirement is a new component, an improvement to an existing application, or tooling for repeat manufacture, our approach begins with understanding the details that determine performance.

Our objective is straightforward: develop practical, manufacturable solutions and support customers with attentive engineering communication.
