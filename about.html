Our company

From tooling knowledge to switch development

Beekay Precision has been making precision press tools and plastic moulds for years. That expertise, together with our knowledge of electrical and electronics technology, now supports the development of industrial switches.

We work with OEMs to develop new switch products or redesign existing applications. Our tooling background helps connect the product design with the parts and manufacturing processes needed to make it.

Beekay pays close attention to customer needs. Our love for detail continues to shape the way we work.
