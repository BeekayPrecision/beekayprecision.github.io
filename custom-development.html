How we can begin

Start with what you have

A complete specification is helpful, but it is not always necessary for the first conversation.

  • 2D drawing or 3D model
  • Existing sample to study
  • Product envelope and interfaces
  • Performance requirement
  • Target volume and timing
  • Problem with a current part

Questions we will explore

What must the component do? What does it connect or interact with? Which features are critical? What environment will it operate in? How will it be assembled? What quantity and product life are expected?

Development path

Structured decisions, visible progress

Discovery and feasibility

Review inputs, application constraints, target quantities and major technical risks.

Concept and quotation

Define a proposed technical route, scope, assumptions, deliverables and commercial stages.

Design and tooling

Develop product details alongside the press tools, moulds, fixtures or assembly process.

Samples and validation

Produce trial parts and incorporate agreed fit, function and dimensional feedback.

Production release

Freeze approved documentation, process steps and quality checkpoints for supply.

New product development

Engineering collaboration for a new electrical or electromechanical component.

Localization

Evaluation of a component for locally supported development, subject to IP and technical review.

Value engineering

Review of an existing design for manufacturability, assembly or tooling improvements.
