Quality approach

Critical features should be defined before they are inspected

A practical quality plan starts with agreed drawings, specifications, samples and acceptance criteria. Inspection can then focus on the characteristics that influence fit, function and safety.

  • Requirement and drawing review
  • Incoming-material verification planning
  • First-off and in-process checks
  • Final inspection against agreed criteria
  • Non-conformance and corrective-action discipline
  • Document and revision awareness

Information to be verified

Certification status, calibrated equipment list, traceability process, test capability, inspection records and applicable product standards.

This page intentionally avoids claiming ISO certification, compliance approvals or test capabilities until supporting records are supplied.

Indicative workflow

Quality gates across development

Requirement agreement

Identify drawings, specifications, critical characteristics and approval expectations.

Tool and sample review

Evaluate trial parts, identify deviations and record required corrections.

Process approval

Agree the production method and inspection checkpoints before regular supply.

Repeat production control

Monitor agreed features and respond systematically to variation or feedback.
